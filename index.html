The Problem

The government tried QR codes. Counterfeiters copied them.

India mandated QR codes on the top 300 medicine brands in 2023. Within two years, fake drug networks were printing working replicas a copied QR scans as "Genuine" every time.

QR Scan Alone
A counterfeiter copies the exact QR from a genuine strip. Your scan says "Genuine." You're fooled and so is everyone else who buys that batch.
Dawai Sahi Hai
We track how many times a batch has been scanned, from where, and what the community reports. A copied code can't fake ten thousand independent scans.
How It Works

Three signals, checked together

No single signal is enough on its own. Together, they're nearly impossible for a counterfeiter to fake.

Database Match
Every scan checks against verified manufacturer batch records. The first filter but not the last one.
Scan Volume Anomaly
A batch of 10,000 strips shouldn't see 80,000 scans. When volume outpaces production, we flag it automatically, no human review needed.
Community Reports
Reports are persisted in the backend and linked to future verification checks. Each validated report adds another signal for the next buyer.
